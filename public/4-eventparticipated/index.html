Event 1: TechMaker Coffee Talk #01

Event 1: TECHMAKER COFFEE TALK #01 — AI FOR USER GROWTH & ACQUISITION

⚠️ Note: The information below is for reference purposes only. Please do not copy it verbatim into your report, including this warning.

Summary Report: “AI for User Growth & Acquisition”

Event Objectives

  • Provide an interactive, intimate offline space for tech and marketing professionals to exchange real-world insights.
  • Explore how AI is transforming user growth and acquisition strategies for marketers, product builders, internal corporate teams, and founders.
  • Bridge the gap between engineering and marketing: help tech personnel understand growth needs while empowering marketers/business owners to leverage AI-driven workflows, automation, and AI agents.
  • Discuss practical concepts including “Vibecode” for non-technical individuals and “The 3rd Brain” in the context of the future of work.

Speakers & Panelists

  • Mr. Bui Quang Tinh Tu – Founder, Envision Nexus | Advisor, Vietnam TechMakers
  • Mr. Do Tuan Anh – Senior Manager Data Engineer, Techcombank | Advisor, Vietnam TechMakers
  • Mr. Jackie Long – Founder, MyRetail

Event Details

  • Organizers: Vietnam TechMakers in collaboration with UAN Marketing Community & AWS Vietnam
  • Sponsor: Techcombank
  • Date & Time: 09:00 AM – 11:00 AM | Wednesday, July 22, 2026
  • Location: AWS Office – Floor 7, Grand Terra Building, 36 Cat Linh, O Cho Dua Ward, Dong Da District, Hanoi

Key Highlights

1. AI & Vibecode as Game-Changers for Growth

  • Growth and acquisition are no longer strictly marketing functions; complex engineering concepts such as automated workflows and AI agents are becoming mainstream growth tools.
  • Examining why AI and “Vibecode” represent a structural shift rather than a temporary trend in scaling user acquisition without requiring large teams.

2. Demystifying “Vibecode”

  • Clarified that Vibecode is not about learning traditional programming syntax, but rather leveraging AI tools to construct functional solutions and automated workflows.
  • Guidance for non-coders on how and where to start utilizing AI for building products and growth pipelines.

3. “The 3rd Brain” Concept

  • Explored how integrating additional AI “brains” into personal and organizational workflows reshapes productivity, job roles, and future work efficiency.

4. Tech & Marketing Cross-Disciplinary Synergy

  • For Tech Professionals: Gained deep context into what growth marketers need from AI models and data pipelines.
  • For Marketers & Business Leaders: Acquired a builder’s mindset from senior data/tech experts to apply technology effectively without over-engineering.

Key Takeaways

Design & Growth Mindset

  • Growth as a Shared Responsibility: User acquisition requires continuous collaboration between technical architecture (data pipelines, AI agents) and marketing execution.
  • Builder’s Mindset for Marketers: Non-technical team members can leverage AI-assisted tools (“Vibecode”) to validate ideas, build prototypes, and automate growth loops rapidly.

Technical & AI Execution

  • AI-Driven Workflows: Replacing manual, multi-step acquisition tasks with autonomous AI agents and automated workflows.
  • Data-Centric Acquisition: Utilizing data engineering principles to measure, optimize, and scale user acquisition channels accurately.

Future of Work Strategy

  • Adopting “The 3rd Brain”: Embracing AI as an extension of cognitive capability to handle routine tasks, allowing teams to focus on strategy and high-value problem solving.

Applying to Work

  • Implement Vibecode & AI Workflows: Experiment with AI tools to automate internal reporting and prototype lightweight growth tools for projects.
  • Enhance Cross-Functional Communication: Apply insights from marketing and data engineering to design systems that better align with business growth metrics.
  • Explore AI Agents: Pilot small-scale AI agent workflows for user engagement and data collection.

Event Experience

Attending TechMaker Coffee Talk #01 at the AWS Vietnam office was a highly rewarding experience. Unlike traditional large-scale conferences, the interactive coffee-talk format fostered candid, multi-dimensional discussions among senior advisors, core community members, and attendees.

Key Experiences Included:

  1. Direct Discussion with Senior Experts: Engaging directly with founders and senior data leaders provided clear perspectives on how top organizations merge technology with growth strategies.
  2. Actionable Insights on Vibecode: Gained a realistic understanding of how non-engineers can build software solutions using AI, demystifying low-code/no-code paradigms.
  3. Networking & Knowledge Exchange: Connected with professionals across tech, data, and marketing backgrounds, reinforcing the importance of cross-domain collaboration.

Lessons Learned:

  • AI levels the playing field, enabling small teams or individual builders to achieve growth outcomes that previously required large departments.
  • Technical architecture should proactively support business growth metrics rather than operating in isolation.
